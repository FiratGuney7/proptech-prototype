Colours and construction

KeyCost orange#ff9600 · 62% of the circle, “Key”
TEKCE blue#1360c9 · 18%
TEKCE light blue#4694ff · 20%
TEKCE navy#133461 · “Cost”
  • The circle is split 62 / 18 / 20, starting at 36° (one o'clock), with three even cuts that stay clear of the keyhole.
  • The two blues always sit together at the top left; don't move or swap the colours.
  • Wordmark: Libre Franklin, “Key” 700 and “Cost” 300, letter-spacing −3.5%; symbol height 1.08× the type size.
  • Minimum size: symbol 16 px, full logo 20 px tall.
  • Keep clear space of at least the keyhole's height around the logo.
  • The downloadable logo SVGs use live text in Libre Franklin; outline the text before sending files outside the web.
