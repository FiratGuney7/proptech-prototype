Peri: ten logo directions

Peri should read as part of TEKCE, never as a separate app, while still feeling like someone, not something. Four main directions, then six wider ideas. Each one is shown as a logo on light and dark, as the chat avatar it will mostly live as, inside a chat, and next to TeleProperty, TeleMortgage and MyTEKCE.

Brainstorm draft, 19.11.2026. Rough sketches for discussion, not final artwork.

Direction 1 · Recommended

The living dot

A plain, bold "Peri" whose only special detail is the dot over the i. All of Peri's character lives in that dot: it is the avatar, it breathes while Peri thinks and turns into the typing dots. The word is set with the Turkish dotless "ı", so the dot is truly Peri's own.

Whichever direction we choose, we avoid:

  • Sparkle iconsThe ✨ symbol is used by almost every AI product; Peri would look like any AI.
  • Purple gradientsSame reason. Peri's colour stays flat and close to TEKCE blue.
  • Faces, robots, fairy figuresClichéd, and pairing the assistant with a female figure is a corporate risk.
  • "AI" in the logoPeri is a team member; "Peri AI" turns Peri into a tool. Fine in body copy, not in the mark.
