Plan my crypto payment

“Can I pay for the property in Bitcoin?”

Antalya — September 2017

A buyer put the question to our chairman, Bayram TEKÇE. There were no established crypto exchanges or payment systems yet. We said yes — and found a way.

  1. 2017

    The first sale

    20 Bitcoins — roughly 80.000 USD at the time — for a large part of an apartment in Antalya. Converted into USD and completed in three days: probably the first property sale ever paid in Bitcoin.

  2. 2020

    Others follow

    Paying for property in crypto has become common, and many developers and agencies now accept it.

  3. Today

    A daily routine

    More than 2.500 sales paid in Bitcoin, Ethereum and USDT — in every country where TEKCE sells.

Three steps are yours. We take care of the rest.

Your crypto transfer is tied to the payment conditions of your sales agreement — from the first conversation to the seller's bank account.

You

  1. 01

    Tell your property consultant

    While choosing the property — before the sales agreement — say you will pay with crypto, which asset and where you are buying.

  2. 02

    Sign the paperwork

    Fill in the Know Your Customer (KYC) form and sign the crypto payment agreement, which ensures your payment reaches the seller's bank account.

  3. 03

    Transfer the crypto

    Send your crypto to TEKCE's company wallet.

TEKCE

  1. 04

    Exchange at market value

    As soon as it arrives, your crypto is exchanged at its actual market value.

  2. 05

    Pay the seller in fiat

    We send the fiat currency to the seller's bank account according to the conditions of your sales agreement.

Plan my crypto payment

Same steps everywhere. A few local rules.

Spain and Türkiye each add their own rules — plan around them from the start. In the UAE, North Cyprus and Montenegro the standard steps apply.

Spain

BTC · ETH · USDC

Crypto payments are harder in Spain than anywhere else we sell. TEKCE was the first real estate company in Spain to finalise one. USDT is not accepted.

Every crypto payment in Spain is conducted under the supervision of lawyers who are experts in crypto payments — bring your questions about the payment agreement into that process before you transfer.

Türkiye

Through the central bank

The conversion limit and the arrival period are separate points: one is about how much can be converted per day, the other about when the money reaches the seller.

United Arab Emirates

The five steps apply as they are — as they do in North Cyprus and Montenegro.

Other cryptocurrencies, such as Litecoin or XRP, can be accepted after talking to your property consultant, when they have high trading volume on exchanges.

Crypto changes how you pay. Not how you buy.

The property, the sales agreement, the seller's payment and the title deed stay exactly as they are. One line changes.

Before the transfer you also fill in the KYC form and sign the crypto payment agreement.

FAQs

Can I buy property with Bitcoin through TEKCE?

Yes. TEKCE accepts Bitcoin for property purchases across its markets, including Spain and Türkiye. Tell your property consultant that you intend to pay with crypto before the sales agreement.

Does the seller receive cryptocurrency?

No. Your crypto is converted at its real market value, and the agreed amount is sent to the seller's bank account in fiat currency according to the sales agreement conditions.

Which cryptocurrencies are accepted?

Outside Spain: BTC, ETH, USDT and USDC; other cryptocurrencies such as Litecoin or XRP can be accepted after talking to your property consultant, when they have high trading volume on exchanges. In Spain: BTC, ETH and USDC.

What do I need to complete before transferring crypto?

Inform your property consultant before the sales agreement, fill in the KYC form and sign the crypto payment agreement under which your payment reaches the seller's bank account.

What limits apply in Türkiye?

The daily crypto conversion limit is 1.000.000 USD. The converted amount arrives by SWIFT in 2 to 3 days in USD and 5 to 7 days in EUR.
