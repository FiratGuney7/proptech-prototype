FAQs

What is TEKCE Proptech?

TEKCE Proptech brings together the technologies TEKCE has developed for international property buyers, sellers and partners: the TEKCE Platform and App, KeyCost, TeleProperty ®, TeleMortgage, MyTEKCE and Peri, our AI.

Can I buy a property in Türkiye without travelling?

Yes. With TeleProperty ®, viewings take place over live video, contracts can be exchanged by scan or post, and the title deed can be transferred by our team under power of attorney.

Are the KeyCost figures exact?

KeyCost figures are calculated on the listing's starting price and are for guidance; current legislation applies. Your advisor can prepare a personal cost breakdown for the exact property you choose.

Where can I talk to Peri today?

Peri is being rolled out step by step. It supports our team in their daily work and has begun welcoming clients in 24/7 live chat, with every TEKCE channel to follow.
