FAQs

What is TEKCE Proptech?

TEKCE Proptech brings together the technologies TEKCE has developed for international property buyers, sellers and partners: our online platforms and the TEKCE App, KeyCost, TeleProperty ®, TeleMortgage, MyTEKCE and Peri, our AI.

Can I buy a property in Türkiye without travelling?

Yes. With TeleProperty ®, viewings take place over live video, contracts can be exchanged by scan or post, and the title deed can be transferred by our team under power of attorney.

Are the KeyCost figures exact?

KeyCost figures are calculated on the listing's starting price and are for guidance; current legislation applies. Your property consultant can prepare a personal cost breakdown for the exact property you choose.

Where can I talk to Peri today?

Peri is live. It supports our team in their daily work and welcomes clients in 24/7 live chat on every TEKCE channel.
