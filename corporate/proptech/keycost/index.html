FAQs

Are KeyCost figures exact?

No — they are a guide. Costs are calculated on the listing's starting price and change with the property you choose. Figures may change, and current legislation always applies.

Why is the percentage different from one listing to another?

Purchase costs depend on the country, the type of property and, in some cases, the nationality of the buyer and the seller. That is why every listing shows its own breakdown.

Do I pay a realtor fee?

The realtor fee is shown as its own line on each listing. On listings where the buyer pays no commission, it is shown as 0 with a footnote.

Can I get a breakdown for my own situation?

Yes. Use “Get your personal cost breakdown” on any listing, and a TEKCE property consultant will prepare the figures for the exact unit and your circumstances.
