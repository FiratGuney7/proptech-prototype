FAQs

Who can use MyTEKCE?

MyTEKCE is for buyers, sellers and business partners of TEKCE. You choose your role when you register at tekce.com/mytekce.

Can I keep track of the properties I've viewed?

Yes. Buyers can save searches and favourite properties, receive alerts for new matching listings, arrange viewing tours, take notes during visits and keep track of viewed properties.

How do sellers follow their sale?

Sellers can see real-time listing views, visit reports with customer visit statistics, and track the progress of their sale and its documents online.

What do partners get?

Partners get access to all properties in the TEKCE database, referral links with real-time lead and deal tracking, legal help from TEKCE's in-house lawyers and a white-label app to present listings under their own brand.

Features as described on tekce.com (“Discover MyTEKCE: The Ultimate Real Estate Dashboard”), the TEKCE Affiliate Program page and the TEKCE partner press release of 26.12.2025.
