FAQs

What is Peri?

Peri is TEKCE's AI. At its core is Peri AI, a corporate knowledge library holding TEKCE's working principles, know-how and way of doing business. Peri officially joined the TEKCE team on 7 August 2026.

Where can I talk to Peri?

Peri is being rolled out step by step. It has begun welcoming clients in 24/7 live chat, and every TEKCE website and chat channel will follow.

Will Peri replace my TEKCE property consultant?

No. Our property consultants remain responsible for welcoming clients and selling. Peri supports them so they are better prepared and faster to answer.

Why the name Peri?

A peri is a being from Turkish tales — intelligent, graceful, sharp in perception, reliable, fast and good.
