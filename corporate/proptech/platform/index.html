FAQs

In which languages is tekce.com available?

tekce.com is available in 12 languages: English, French, Russian, Arabic, Turkish, Chinese, German, Spanish, Polish, Swedish, Dutch and Persian. Our advisors speak more than 30 languages.

Can I see prices in my own currency?

Yes. Prices can be shown in EUR, USD, GBP, RUB, BTC, TRY, AED, CNY, PLN, SEK, SAR and CAD.

Where can I download the TEKCE App?

The TEKCE App installs directly from your browser: scan the QR code on tekce.com, open the app and choose “Add to Home Screen”.

How do property alerts work?

Save your search in the TEKCE App and choose to be notified of new matching listings by push notification, SMS or email.
