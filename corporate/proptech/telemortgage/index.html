Four steps. Three of them from home.

TeleMortgage is designed so that almost everything happens remotely. The final Know Your Customer signature is the one step that takes place at a TEKCE office.

  1. Select your property

    Browse TEKCE listings or let our experts help you find a property — including properties offered with installment sales.

  2. Submit your documents

    Once the sales terms are agreed, send us four documents: a valid passport, your tax identification number, proof of address and an income statement.

  3. Application processing

    TEKCE starts the mortgage application for you and informs you of the result — in as little as 24 hours.

  4. KYC signing

    You sign the Know Your Customer documents at a TEKCE office. It is the only step of TeleMortgage that takes place in person.

FAQs

What is TeleMortgage?

TeleMortgage is TEKCE's remote mortgage system for international buyers. TEKCE prepares and submits your mortgage application and informs you of the result, so you don't have to deal with banks and offices abroad yourself.

Which documents do I need?

A valid passport, your tax identification number, proof of address and an income statement.

How long does it take?

Once your documents are complete, you can receive the result of your application in as little as 24 hours.

Do I need to visit an office?

Yes, once: the Know Your Customer (KYC) documents are signed at a TEKCE office. All other steps can be completed remotely.

Is my mortgage guaranteed?

No. Every application is assessed by the lending bank, and loan terms are set by the bank. TEKCE prepares and follows the application on your behalf.
