Context · Türkiye

Türkiye's land registry has gone digital, too.

Remote purchasing in Türkiye is supported by online services of the General Directorate of Land Registry and Cadastre (TKGM).

Web Tapu

TKGM's online application portal. Sale applications can be started without visiting the land registry office, with a dedicated entry for foreigners.

e-Randevu

Online appointment booking at land registry offices.

e-Tahsilat

Online payment of land registry fees, with a reference number sent by SMS.

These are public services of TKGM, not TEKCE products. The title deed signing itself still takes place at the land registry office — in person or by power of attorney.

FAQs

How can I buy property in Türkiye remotely?

The whole purchase can be done remotely. It starts with an analysis of your preferences, followed by a personalised online viewing tour, negotiation, a written sales agreement, payment and the title deed transfer.

Which apps are used for the viewing tour?

Viewings take place over FaceTime, WhatsApp or Skype — whichever you prefer. You can ask the seller questions directly during the tour.

How is the sales agreement signed?

Either the original stays at our office and a scanned copy is emailed to you, or the originals are posted to you; you sign and send back the seller's copy.

Do I have to travel for the title deed?

No. You can collect the title deed in person, or our agents can complete the transfer on your behalf under power of attorney.

Can I reserve a property remotely?

Yes. Once a deposit is paid, the unit is taken off the market — useful for time-limited offers and fixing the price before increases.
